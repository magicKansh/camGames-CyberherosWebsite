Hero handbook

Everything the games teach, on one page. Read it before you fight, or after you lose.

Use strong passwords

  • Long beats clever. Aim for at least 16 characters. A random string or a passphrase of 4–5 unrelated words both work.
  • Use a different password for every account, so one leak doesn't unlock everything.
  • Let a password manager create and remember them. You only memorize one.
  • Skip names, birthdays, pets and teams. Villains check those first.

Turn on MFA

  • Multifactor authentication adds a second check after your password: an app code, a security key, a fingerprint or face scan, or a passkey.
  • Even if a villain steals your password, they get stuck at the second lock.
  • Turn it on first for email, banking and social media.
  • Authenticator apps, security keys and passkeys are stronger than text-message codes. Never share a code with anyone who asks for it.

Recognize and report phishing

  • Red flags: urgency or threats, prizes that are too good to be true, unexpected attachments, odd sender addresses, and requests for passwords, codes or money.
  • Hover over or long-press a link to see where it really goes. When in doubt, open the app or type the site address yourself.
  • Check with the sender another way, like calling a number you already know.
  • Report it with your email's "Report phishing" button or to your school or work IT, forward scam texts to 7726 (SPAM), then delete it.

Update your software

  • Updates patch security holes that attackers already know about.
  • Turn on automatic updates for your phone, computer, browser and apps.
  • Install updates soon. "Remind me later" leaves the door open.
  • Only update from official places like app stores and settings menus. A pop-up saying your software is out of date can be a trap.
